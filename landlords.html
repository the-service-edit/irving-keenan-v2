What to expect when we manage your property.

A clear view of how we select tenants, inspect, maintain and report, so you always know where things stand.

Finding the right tenant

We advertise across the major portals and with signage, conduct inspections, and assess every application carefully: checking rental references, employment and income, and national tenancy databases where appropriate. Applications are now completed online, so you receive complete, well-organised applications faster.

Starting the tenancy

We prepare the tenancy agreement, lodge the security bond, and complete a detailed property condition report with photographs. Tenants have seven days to review and return it.

Inspections

The first routine inspection is carried out at around six weeks, then at regular intervals, always with the written notice required by law. You receive a report after each inspection.

Rent and reviews

Rent is collected and disbursed with monthly electronic statements and a yearly summary. We review rent against the market, within the limits of WA law (currently no more than one increase in any 12-month period).

Pets

Under WA law tenants can ask to keep a pet, and landlords can only refuse or set conditions on specific reasonable grounds within 14 days. We manage these requests for you and explain your options.

Maintenance

We coordinate repairs with trusted local tradespeople and keep you informed. Urgent repairs are actioned promptly, within the timeframes WA law requires.

Ask for a rental appraisal or a copy of our management agreement.
