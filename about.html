A family business on Beaufort Street for more than four decades.

Irving & Keenan is a family owned and operated boutique real estate agency specialising in sales, auctioneering and property management in Mount Lawley and our neighbouring suburbs.

We are “A Part of Mount Lawley”.

Together with our staff, we’ve built a high profile on a simple company philosophy: provide superior service. That has earned us an enviable reputation, supported by continued repeat and referral business.

Our directors, sales consultants, property managers and office team bring outstanding experience and extensive local knowledge, an edge that is vital in achieving the best results for our clients.

Today the agency is led by principals Stuart Irving and his son Brad Irving, a second-generation director who has lived or worked in real estate for most of his life.

We invite you to visit us at 674 Beaufort Street, Mount Lawley, to discuss any aspect of your real estate needs.
