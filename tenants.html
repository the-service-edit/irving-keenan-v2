Renting with Irving & Keenan.

Everything you need during your tenancy: how to apply, how to report a repair, and what happens when you move out.

Apply for a rentalReport a repair

Applying

Please view the property before applying. You can then apply online. Every adult who will live at the property completes their own section. Applications usually take 36–48 hours to process.

Move-in costs are generally a security bond of four weeks’ rent plus two weeks’ rent in advance. If a pet is approved, a pet bond may also apply.

Paying rent

Rent is paid in advance. Your property manager will give you payment details in writing at the start of your tenancy. Payment details never change by email. If you receive a message asking you to pay into a different account, call us on (08) 9272 0522 before paying.

Inspections

Your first routine inspection is usually at around six weeks, then at regular intervals. We always give written notice as required by law. You don’t need to be home.

Repairs and maintenance

  • Urgent repairs, such as a gas leak, burst water pipe or serious roof leak: phone the office immediately on (08) 9272 0522. Outside business hours, follow the emergency instructions in your tenancy documents.
  • Priority repairs, such as a broken hot water system: phone or submit a request below with as much detail as possible.
  • Routine repairs, such as an appliance fault: submit a request below.

Please report any damage promptly, ideally within three days.

Pets

If you’d like to keep a pet during your tenancy, ask us for the approved WA pet request form. Assistance animals don’t need permission.

Moving out

For a periodic tenancy, give at least 21 days’ written notice. If your fixed term is ending and you don’t plan to stay, let us know in writing. We’ll send you a vacating checklist to help you get your bond back smoothly.
