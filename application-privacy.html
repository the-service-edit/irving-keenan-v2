Application collection notice

How we collect, use and share the information you give us when you apply for a rental property.

Draft for legal review. This wording hasn’t yet been approved by Irving & Keenan or its advisers. Version DRAFT-2026-09-17.

Who is collecting your information

Irving & Keenan Real Estate Pty Ltd, 674 Beaufort Street, Mount Lawley WA 6050, as property manager acting for the lessor.

Why we collect it

To verify your identity, assess your application on the lessor’s behalf, and, if your application is successful, prepare and manage your tenancy. If you don’t give us information we ask for, we may not be able to process your application.

What we collect

Your contact details, current and previous addresses and rental history, employment and income information, referee details, household and pet information, and the documents you upload (photo identification and proof of income). We only ask for what we need to assess the application.

Who we may share it with

The lessor; the referees, landlords, property managers and employers you list; residential tenancy database operators; and service providers who host our systems in Australia under confidentiality obligations. If you become a tenant, information may later be shared as described in our privacy policy. We don’t sell your information and we don’t use it for marketing without your consent.

How we protect it

Applications are submitted over an encrypted connection and stored encrypted in Australia. Documents are never emailed and are only accessible to authorised staff. Access is logged.

How long we keep it

If your application is unsuccessful or withdrawn, we delete it and your documents after 90 days. Unfinished applications are deleted after 30 days of inactivity. If you become a tenant, we keep it for as long as the law requires.

Access and correction

You can ask to see or correct your information at any time. Use “Send a message” in your application, or call (08) 9272 0522. You can also complain to us, and then to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Notice of use of residential tenancy databases

Residential Tenancies Act 1987 (WA), section 82C.

It is our usual practice to use one or more residential tenancy databases to check an applicant’s tenancy history. This notice is given whether or not we search for your details.
