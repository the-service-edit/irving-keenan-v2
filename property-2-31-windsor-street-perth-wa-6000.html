Inner city living at its best

A stylish, light and bright townhouse in a small, secure, well-maintained complex of only six.

Neutral tones, timber flooring to the living areas and carpet to three great-sized upstairs bedrooms, all with built-in robes. A modernised kitchen with gas hotplates and dishwasher, and a downstairs powder room for entertaining.

Open-plan meals and living with a reverse-cycle split system, overlooking a private, easy-care courtyard with no lawn. The main bedroom has its own split system and ensuite. Spacious double garage with plenty of storage.

Minutes from public transport, with the CBD, TAFE, schools, parks, cafés and shops a short trip away.
