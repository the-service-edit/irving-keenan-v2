Urban chic

From the gorgeous street appeal to polished concrete bench tops, this home blends an original cottage with a modern bespoke renovation, bringing together city-fringe living, graceful character and modern luxuries.

Key period features are kept, including two ornate fireplaces in the bedrooms, alongside hand-built polished concrete and Jarrah benchtops, American Black Walnut cabinetry and landscaped outdoor entertaining with a built-in BBQ.

Tuck-pointed façade, timber porch and leadlight entry. Three large bedrooms, the main with a custom double-vanity ensuite, plus a stunning second bathroom.

In a quiet no-through street, a minute’s walk to Beaufort Street shops, cafés and bars, Hyde Park, Birdwood Park, city buses and bike paths. Please note: parking is verge parking.
