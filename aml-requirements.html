AML/CTF requirements

From 1 July 2026, new requirements under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act apply to real estate services.

These reforms are designed to strengthen Australia’s financial system by helping prevent serious financial crime. Real estate businesses must now undertake customer due diligence and verify the identity of clients involved in certain property transactions. We’re committed to making this as straightforward, secure and efficient as possible.

What this means for you

If you’re buying or selling with Irving & Keenan, we may need to collect and verify information before we can proceed. Depending on your transaction, we may ask for:

  • Proof of identity
  • Confirmation of address
  • Information about directors, shareholders, trustees, beneficiaries or others who own or control a company, trust or other entity
  • Information about the source of funds or source of wealth, where the legislation requires it
  • Additional documents where needed to meet our legal obligations

Transactions that may need verification

  • Buying and selling property
  • Property auctions
  • Other property-related services where required by law

How we verify you

We may ask you to complete verification using FirstAML, the secure digital platform recommended and nominated by REIWA. You can upload documents and verify online, reducing paperwork and delays. Information collected for AML/CTF purposes is handled securely and only where required by law.

Frequently asked questions

I’ve worked with you before. Why do I need to provide ID now?

Australian law now requires real estate businesses to verify clients before providing certain services, even if we’ve worked together before.

What if I’m buying or selling through a company or trust?

We may need extra information to identify the people who own or control that entity.

Will this delay my transaction?

For most clients verification is quick. Providing information promptly helps avoid delays.

Does this apply to renting?

Not currently. Standard residential rentals don’t generally require AML/CTF verification. If that changes, we’ll let you know.

What if I don’t provide the information?

We may be unable to proceed with your transaction until our verification obligations are met. If you have concerns, please talk to us and we’ll explain why the information is needed.

More information is available from AUSTRAC (austrac.gov.au).
