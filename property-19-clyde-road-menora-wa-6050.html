Character and charm, spacious family home

A well-presented family home in a sought-after neighbourhood overlooking the Alexander Park Tennis Club, combining original character with modern enhancements.

Ornate ceilings, cornices and woodwork, leadlight doors and windows, and Jarrah floorboards. An open-plan kitchen with a 900mm upright gas stove, dishwasher and generous storage.

Three generous bedrooms with built-in or walk-in robes, plus a detached multi-purpose room with its own bathroom. Formal lounge and dining with a gas fireplace, and a huge undercover alfresco overlooking the fully enclosed yard and below-ground pool.

A tree-lined neighbourhood surrounded by reserves and parks, a short ride or stroll to Coolbinia Primary School and Mount Lawley Senior High School.
